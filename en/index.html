What Atarazana House is

Small, regular meetups for the people building technology in Málaga.

Málaga already has talent, companies and big events. What was missing was something closer: a meetup you come back to regularly, where little by little you start recognising faces.

Each meetup brings together around 60 people in a different place in the city. There are demos of real products, the odd technical talk and plenty of time to talk. Entry is free and by invitation.

Who it is for

For anyone who is building something, whatever their job title.
  • Founders
  • Software engineers
  • Research and AI
  • Product
  • Design
  • Early startup employees
  • Students already building

It does not matter whether you are just starting or have built several companies. The only thing we look at is what you are doing and what you can bring to the conversation.

What happens at a meetup

Not every meetup has everything. Each one combines the formats that fit who is coming and what there is to show.

  1. Live demos

    Someone shows their product working, in front of everyone. No slides and no recorded videos. If something breaks, it breaks live, and that is usually the best part.

  2. Technical talk

    Someone explains how something is built on the inside: the architecture, the decisions, what it costs per month and what they learned when it broke. Ask anything.

  3. Small tables

    A few people and one concrete topic: finding a cofounder, first hires, selling abroad or knowing when to change course. What is said at the table stays there.

  4. Build nights

    Laptops open and nobody presenting. You come with an idea or a task and leave with something done: a prototype, an experiment or a commit.

  5. Afterwards

    When the programme ends, people stay. Food, drinks and time to keep talking: often the best part of the night.

How we do it

Show, don't sell

This is where working products get shown. Talks are given by people who have built something, not by sales teams.

Coming back matters

We prefer small, frequent meetups to one big event a year: that way you see the same people again and relationships grow.

From Málaga

You can build a world-class company without leaving. We want the people doing it to have somewhere to meet each other.

Invitation, not status

We ask for invites to look after the mix in the room, not to be exclusive. Inside, it does not matter if you are junior or a serial founder.

Who pays for it

Getting in is free. Costs are covered by partner companies.

Partners pay for the venue, food and drinks at each meetup. They do not buy demos, talks or invites: who gets in and what gets shown is decided by the community.

Why this name

Different tools, same attitude.

The Atarazanas were Málaga’s shipyards in Nasrid times: the place where ships were built. The gate of that building still stands and is now the entrance to the Atarazanas Market.

We kept the idea of a workshop where things get built, with no stage. Today what gets built in Málaga is products, companies and technology; the attitude is the same.

Frequently asked questions

Where is it?
Each meetup happens in a different place in Málaga: an office, a workshop, a terrace… The exact address comes with the invite.
When is the next one?
The first one, Session 001, will be in autumn 2026. As soon as there is a date we will let everyone signed up on Luma know.
How much does it cost?
Nothing. Entry is free; costs are covered by partners.
Do I have to be a founder?
No. Founders, engineers, research, product and design people and early startup employees all come. We look at what you are building, not your job title.
Do I have to present something?
No. Most people come to watch demos, ask questions and talk. If you want to show something, ask for a demo slot: it does not need to be finished.
Why do I need an invite?
Because places are few (around 60) and we want a varied room of people who are building. We do not ask for a CV or a famous profile: a link to what you do is enough.
Can I bring someone?
Invites are individual because the room is small. If you know someone who fits, they can request an invite, or write to us telling us who they are and what they are building.
What language is spoken?
Spanish and English, depending on who is at the table. Many demos are in English.

Tell us what you are building.

Request an invite on Luma with a link to what you are working on: your website, GitHub, product or a post. No CV and no pitch deck. We will let you know as soon as Session 001 has a date.
