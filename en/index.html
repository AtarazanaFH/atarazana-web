The concept

Málaga already has talent, companies and ambition. What was missing was a place to come back to.

Big events put a city on the map. What builds an ecosystem are the relationships born between one event and the next. La Atarazana exists so that those conversations don't depend on a date on the calendar: a house open often enough for faces to stop being strangers.

Every city where things happen has a place like this. What makes it good is never the stage: it's that you always end up talking to someone who interests you. That's what we're building in Málaga.

Why La Atarazana

Same attitude. Only the craft changed.

In Málaga's Atarazanas, ships were built: the ones that sailed the Mediterranean from this port. The name given to their builders even survives in the market that still remembers them. We're not trying to recreate that history; what interests us is the attitude of people who make things with their hands, without asking for permission.

Today that energy lives in the people creating products, companies and technology from here. We borrow the name; the rest we write together.

What happens inside

Five formats. Each session runs the ones it needs, so no two nights are the same.

  1. Launch

    You show your product working, live. No slides, no recorded videos. If something breaks, it breaks in front of everyone, and that's usually the best part.

  2. Below the waterline

    The decisions that almost never make it into a talk: architecture, trade-offs, how much it costs a month and what you learned after breaking production. You can ask anything.

  3. Navigators' table

    One table, few people and plenty of time. The conversations that rarely happen in front of a hundred attendees: real numbers, hiring and when to change course.

  4. Build nights

    Laptops open, nobody presenting. You walk in with an idea and leave having shipped something, even if it's small. This is where building happens in the room.

  5. The tide

    The program ends and the conversation doesn't. Often the best part of the night happens after the last demo.

The dock

Show, don't tell

Ideas are interesting; working products more so. If you can show it, show it: demos go first and keynotes come from people who have already built something.

The same faces

Every time you come back you recognize more people, and that's where relationships that last begin. That's why the house cares about frequency before capacity.

Málaga, no complexes

You can build a first-class company here without asking for permission and without moving to another city. The dock exists so that the people doing it have somewhere to return.

Guest list

How to become a partner

Before you come

Where is the dock?
There's no fixed address. Each session happens in a different place in Málaga: an office, a workshop, a borrowed terrace. What repeats isn't the place, it's the people; the exact address is always in the invitation.
Do I need to be a founder?
No. Founder isn't about having started a company; it's a way of working: you take a problem, treat it as your own and drive it forward. In the room there are engineers, researchers, product people, designers and first employees. The only thing we look at is what you're building.
How much does it cost to get in?
Nothing. The door is free, and what the night costs, from the room to the last beer, is covered by the partners.
Do I have to present something?
No. Most of the room comes to watch, to ask and to stay for the tide. If you're building something, ask for a demo slot: it doesn't have to be finished, it almost never is.
Can I bring someone?
Tell us who they are and what they're building. The room is small and every name takes a seat, so invitations are individual; if they fit, they get in just like you.
What language is spoken?
Whatever is needed. Spanish, English and Andalusian mix depending on who's at the table, and many demos run in English because the product is already out there. Switching mid-sentence is normal and no one minds.

Tell us what you're building.

A link to what you've done is enough; no CV and no pitch deck. We'll write to you as soon as Session 001 has a date.
