For companies

No partners, no meetups.

Entry is free for everyone. Partners pay for the venue, food and drinks at each meetup.

How it works

Atarazana Founder House is always free for attendees. To make that possible, some companies cover the costs of each meetup: the venue, food and drinks.

In return, they visibly support the Málaga tech community without turning the meetup into a commercial event.

What you get

  • Your brand on the website and at the venue of the meetup you sponsor.
  • Presence in a room of Málaga founders, engineers and product people who are building.
  • A partners’ dinner with the organising team and some of the attendees, with no sales presentations.
  • People on your team who are building can request an invite like anyone else, under the same criteria.

What cannot be bought

To keep the meetups worth attending, some things are not for sale:

  • A demo slot.
  • A talk or keynote.
  • Invites for specific people.
  • Deciding who gets in.

Options and pricing

You can sponsor a single meetup or support us for the whole year. We do not publish prices because they depend on the venue and what each meetup needs: write to us and we will tell you.

Getting started

Write to atarazanafh@gmail.com telling us who you are, what your company does and what you are interested in. We will reply with the available options.
