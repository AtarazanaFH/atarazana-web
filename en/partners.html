For companies and studios

No partners, no launch.

Sessions are free. Someone pays for the room, the bar and the chairs.

The rule

The only thing we hold is the door: free for those who come in, and free for those who come back. Partners pay for the room, the bar and the chairs. They don't pay for appearances, they don't pay for speakers, they don't pay for the mic. The conversation is not advertising space.

What you get

  • Your name on the session's website and on a panel in the room.
  • A table with people making decisions and building the next ones.
  • A partners dinner with the team and part of the guest list, no pitch and no agenda.
  • If your people build, they can request access like anyone else. The guest list is the same for everyone.

What can't be bought

  • You can't buy a demo slot.
  • You can't buy a keynote.
  • You can't buy a person's access to the guest list.
  • You can't buy the right to decide who is in the room.

Formats

One session, or the year. We talk one session at a time and about what happens in between. The price is not published on purpose: it depends on the room, the bar and what's already in Málaga's ecosystem.
