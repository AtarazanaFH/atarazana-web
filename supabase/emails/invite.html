You came to a marea, so we are inviting you to La Tripulación: the private part of the Atarazana website where you see who was at the same marea as you. It is there to keep in touch after each meetup. Access is limited to people who have attended a marea and the organising team. Nobody outside the community can access La Tripulación or see the attendee list or profiles. You will only see people who were at a marea with you and have also chosen to join, and they will see you. It is optional: if you want in, fill in your profile. We want what starts at a marea to carry on between meetups. So during a marea you do not have to worry about getting everyone's details and can focus on the conversation. If you hit it off with someone, you will not lose track of them.
